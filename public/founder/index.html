02 / DEVGANGA AMRIT

A brand inspired by Devprayag.

DEVGANGA AMRIT is a spiritual heritage project inspired by the natural setting and spiritual significance of Devprayag, Uttarakhand. Its proposed product concept is Ganga Jal for puja and religious rituals, with clear information and careful presentation at the centre of the experience.

The website, visual identity, and product imagery are being developed as part of that direction. The bottle artwork shown on the main site is a concept visual; it does not confirm commercial availability.

03 / THE PLACE THAT INSPIRES THE BRAND

Devprayag.
Where the rivers meet.

At Devprayag, the Bhagirathi and Alaknanda rivers meet to form the Ganga. The place gives DEVGANGA AMRIT its point of reference and its sense of connection to a living heritage.
